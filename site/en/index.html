FAQ

Can I get banned?

No. Game State Integration is an official Dota 2 feature by Valve. The app doesn't inject into the game, read its memory or control your hero; the advice is like a friend's on Discord.

Is it really free?

Yes, completely. The AI review needs your own free Google Gemini key (takes a minute to get); everything else works the same without it.

I can't see the advice over the game

Windows doesn't draw windows over exclusive fullscreen. In Dota: Settings → Video → “Borderless window”. Or turn on the voice — it is heard in any mode.

Which heroes get advice?

Full advice (farm, items, objectives, abilities) for 64 heroes: carries, mids, offlaners and supports. On any other hero the coach helps you survive (low HP, death streaks, disables, mana, buyback) and gives map timers and tips for your role. Match reviews and progress work for every hero.

Where does the match history come from?

The app records every match by itself and fills it in with OpenDota data. For that, Expose Public Match Data must be on in Dota 2.

Do I need the internet?

Not for advice during the game. The internet is needed for the OpenDota history, the AI review and updates.

Windows says it “protected your PC”

The installer isn't code-signed yet. Click “More info” → “Run anyway”. The code is open, so you can check what's inside.
