Counter items for your hero

Any carry against Phantom Assassin used to get Monkey King Bar. Now the answer depends on the hero you play.

  • Against evasion: Naga Siren takes Bloodthorn, since her illusions never miss the silenced target either; Slark and Spectre take Eye of Skadi first; Drow Ranger needs nothing, as Marksmanship procs never miss.
  • Against illusions: Medusa goes Maelstrom, Mjollnir, then Aghanim's Scepter; Terrorblade and Morphling take Butterfly, then turn the Dragon Lance into Hydra's Breath; Juggernaut, Anti-Mage and Ursa skip Maelstrom, Battle Fury already cleaves.
  • Against Bristleback, Spectre or Huskar: Silver Edge; Phantom Assassin takes Aghanim's Scepter for Fan of Knives; Sven, Ursa and Anti-Mage do without it.
  • Against heavy healing (Huskar, Alchemist, Necrophos…): Eye of Skadi for every carry who has it in the build.
  • Every tip says why that item, in English and Ukrainian.

Build, tempo and skills

Numbers from OpenDota league matches of the current patch, mechanics from Valve's 7.41f data.

  • The usual build follows the order of the won games on that hero.
  • Tempo: when your net worth is over the line where the hero wins about 85% of games (Terrorblade: 12k at minute 20), the counters wait and Wardly follows the tempo build.
  • Skill builds and talents come from the same games; Kez's from 34 games of Yatoro and Satanic, since his two stances share the skill points.

When the game is on the line

Only what the game shows; the call is yours and your team's.

  • «Enemy Aegis until 30:03» when they take Roshan, and keep your buyback gold from minute 20 with their Aegis or your base open.
  • The death screen says how much the buyback was short, and Linken's or BKB by whether you were held in a Hex or in stuns.
  • «Fight at your T2: 3 allies, 2 enemies — TP ready» and «5 enemies together: do not go there alone».
