Wardly 0.58: a plan of their own for mids and offlaners

19 more heroes with a plan of their own: counter items for the enemy draft, the build, tempo and skills from league matches on that hero. Every carry, mid and offlaner Wardly supports now has one.

Offlane: your own BKB instead of Pipe

The advice against magic now depends on what the hero really builds.

  • Axe, Legion Commander, Mars, Primal Beast, Slardar and Timbersaw take their own BKB earlier against three or more magic damage heroes: it is in their build in 50-86% of games.
  • Centaur keeps Pipe of Insight: with it he wins 56% of games against magic, 34% without.
  • Pangolier needs neither: Rolling Thunder gives debuff immunity and 80% magic resistance.
  • Offlaners are no longer told to buy Spirit Vessel against healing: in high ranks the support buys it.

Mid: an answer for the hero

Every item's mechanics checked against Valve's 7.41f data.

  • Queen of Pain, Puck, Void Spirit and Storm Spirit take Linken's against Phantom Assassin and Spirit Breaker: Phantom Strike and Charge of Darkness are cast on you, and it blocks them. Not against Slark's Pounce.
  • Shadow Fiend and Templar Assassin take Monkey King Bar against evasion; Dragon Knight takes Orchid against escapes and Bloodthorn against evasion.
  • Ember Spirit and Void Spirit finish Spirit Vessel early against heavy healing: it cuts health restoration by 70%.
  • Caster mids are no longer told to buy Silver Edge: it is not in their games.

Build, tempo and skills

Numbers from OpenDota league matches of the current patch.

  • The usual build follows the order of the won games on that hero, the tempo build comes when your net worth is over the line where the hero wins about 85% of games.
  • Skill builds and talents come from the same games; Shadow Fiend's three Shadowrazes count as one ability, Dragon Knight and Outworld Destroyer use 7.41 games only, since their abilities changed.
  • Axe now levels Battle Hunger second: that order wins more (55% against 49%).
