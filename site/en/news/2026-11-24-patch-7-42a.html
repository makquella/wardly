Global Mechanics

  • Vector-targeted abilities that are reflected will now reflect in the opposite direction of the initial cast (Walrus Kick, Nightmare, and Rebound. Dead Shot already did this)
  • Damaging neutral creeps while under the effect of Smoke of Deceit will now make them attempt to flee
