Spell Power Reduction

  • Decreased most item contributions to ability scaling, including Spell Amplification, Area of Effect, and Cast Range
  • Decreased damage of the majority of Magic and Pure damage spells
  • Decreased duration of some long duration slows
  • Specifics for any of these changes can be found in the corresponding ability or item notes

Item Potency

  • Attacks from heroes (and hero-like units, including illusions) now have an attribute called Item Potency
  • Some properties of items that apply on-hit effects, like Desolator or Eye of Skadi, are now scaled by the attack’s Item Potency

    For example, the amount of armor reduced by Orb of Blight or the stun chance of Skull Basher

  • The default Item Potency value for an attack is 100%
  • When debuffs are applied from multiple sources, whichever source had the highest Item Potency will be used
  • Examples
  • Any hero attacking with a Tier 4 Evolved Enchantment has +20% Item Potency, so if they have a Desolator the attack would reduce armor by 7.2

    6 * 120%

  • Attacks from Pangolier’s Swashbuckle now have 60% Item Potency, so if he has a Desolator the strikes would reduce armor by 3.6

    6 * 60%

  • Illusions have a default Item Potency of 20% and can now apply most item on hit effects

    Excluding Skull Basher and a few other items. Specific details can be found in the associated item and ability changes

  • 0:00-29:59: Every 5 minutes, 1 siege creep spawns per wave
  • 30:00-59:59: Every 5 minutes, 2 siege creeps spawn per wave

  • 0:00-29:59: Every 5 minutes, 1 siege creep spawns per wave
  • 30:00-59:59: Every 2.5 minutes, 1 siege creep spawns per wave
  • 60:00+: Every 2.5 minutes, 2 siege creeps spawn per wave

Terrain Changes

Twin Gates

  • Twin Gates moved to the top and bottom rim of the map, where small flooded camps used to be
  • The Radiant one is in the exact position where that camp used to be, while Dire one is located above it, on the rim
  • Moved small flooded camps at the top/bottom rims closer to stream ends
  • Moved medium non-flooded camps at the end of streams to the highground that was previously occupied by the Twin Gates

Mid Lane

  • Moved Radiant and Dire Tier 2 tower roughly 260 units away from Radiant base and Radiant and Dire Tier 1 towers roughly 350 units away from Radiant base
  • This equalizes the distances from the respective Radiant/Dire bases to the corresponding tiers of towers

    Previously, the distance between the Radiant highground and Radiant Tier 2 was significantly shorter than between Dire highground and Dire Tier 2, whereas now they are closer in length

  • To accommodate the move, reworked the geometry of the river where mid lanes meet, and moved Radiant mid wave creep spawn to be in front of the Radiant Tier 3 tower, as was already the case with the Dire mid wave spawn
  • Cleaned up the trees by Dire Mid Tier 1 tower and did another pass on the cliff line on the radiant side
  • Added some trees to the sides of Mid lane where they meet to break some lines of sight
  • Radiant Tier 1 tower moved ever so slightly to the south
  • Added more trees to the north of Radiant Tier 1 tower, and narrowed down the lane width where the Tier 1 tower is
  • Slightly increased the amount of highground to the north of the Radiant midlane ramp

Miscellaneous

  • Removed a bunch of trees on the cliff overlooking the stream near the Safelane Tier 1 Towers
  • Spawn boxes of the Safelane Tier 1 small and large camps have been decreased
  • Y'ai 'ng'ngah, Shuggoth h'ee. Shuggoth fhtagn. Shuggoth ftaghu
  • Slightly rearranged the trees around Radiant Safelane Tier 1 small camp
