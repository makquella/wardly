Templar Assassin

Templar Assassin is a ranged mid who plays for tempo. These are the numbers Wardly uses on this hero during the match: how many last hits, when to back off and what saves you.

Below the lower number, the coach tells you during the match and shows a good pace for that minute.

When to back off

  • Under 50% HP the coach tells you to leave; under 35% it is urgent and comes at once.
  • Your save: Refraction. The coach names it when it is ready and reminds you if you died without pressing it.

In a fight

  • Your key fight ability: Meld.
  • Coach's tip: Without Refraction charges she is fragile: back off and let it come back.

What else Wardly tells you

  • Last-hit checks at 5:00 and 8:00 and a Bottle reminder.
  • Runes 20 seconds ahead, and from level 6 when to go to a side lane.
  • The next item of the build and the gold still needed for it.
  • After the match — a review: a score, a map of your deaths, the build against OpenDota statistics.
