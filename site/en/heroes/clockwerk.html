Support

Clockwerk

Clockwerk is a soft support who starts the fights. These are the numbers Wardly uses on this hero during the match: how many last hits, when to back off and what saves you.

Last-hit pace

MinuteLast hits

30:0044–52

A support does not need many last hits: the numbers are a guide, not a goal. If you start taking the carry's creeps, the coach reminds you.

When to back off

  • Under 50% HP the coach tells you to leave; under 35% it is urgent and comes at once.
  • No save of your own: the coach names a ready item — Force Staff, Blink Dagger, Black King Bar, Ghost Scepter, Magic Wand.

In a fight

  • Your key fight ability: Power Cogs, Hookshot.
  • Coach's tip: Hookshot starts the fight: go when your team is close enough to follow.

What else Wardly tells you

  • Stacks at :53 and small-camp pulls in the safe lane, 10 seconds ahead.
  • No wards, or a ward kept in the bag too long — a reminder to place it.
  • No save item by minute 12 — a hint for Glimmer Cape or Force Staff.
  • After the match — a review: a score, a map of your deaths, the build against OpenDota statistics.
