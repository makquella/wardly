Monkey King

Monkey King is a carry who starts fighting early. These are the numbers Wardly uses on this hero during the match: how many last hits, when to back off and what saves you.

Below the lower number, the coach tells you during the match and shows a good pace for that minute.

When to back off

  • Under 50% HP the coach tells you to leave; under 35% it is urgent and comes at once.
  • Your save: Mischief. The coach names it when it is ready and reminds you if you died without pressing it.

In a fight

  • Your key fight ability: Wukong's Command.
  • Coach's tip: Avoid committing when mobility back out is unavailable.

What else Wardly tells you

  • A plan from the pick: a last-hit target for 10:00 and the key item.
  • The next item of the build and the gold still needed for it.
  • No TP for a minute — a reminder. After minute 30 it keeps your buyback gold.
  • After the match — a review: a score, a map of your deaths, the build against OpenDota statistics.
