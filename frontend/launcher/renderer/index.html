        Updates

        Your matches

        Keep your history when you change computers

        Match history

        Move to another computer Installed Wardly on a new computer? Move your matches and reviews there in a minute.
        1. Here, press “Get a code”.
        2. On the new computer: Wardly → Settings → Move to another computer → “I have a code”, and type it in.
        Spare copy in a file
        Once a week and before each update Wardly keeps a copy of your history on this computer: the last 4, up to 200 MB. Restoring only adds what is missing and never removes anything.
